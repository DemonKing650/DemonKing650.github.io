Community Standards

Clear rules. Fair enforcement. Better ARK.

These rules protect the long-term health of the ZoD Varynth cluster. Joining the server means agreeing to follow them on every connected map.

Read the Rules Open Discord
Official Rules

Help keep the cluster strong.

Staff may act on behavior that clearly harms the server or community even when a specific situation is not listed word-for-word below.

!

Use common sense

Rules are not loophole challenges. Attempts to technically avoid the wording while still harming players may be treated as a violation.

01

Respect Every Player

Keep the cluster welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeted bullying.
  • Keep disagreements civil and move serious disputes to staff.
  • Do not impersonate staff members or other players.
02

No Griefing or Sabotage

Do not intentionally damage another player's experience.

  • No blocking bases, caves, terminals, obelisks, or important resources.
  • Do not drag hostile creatures into another player's base.
  • Do not steal from unsecured areas simply because the game allows it.
03

Build Responsibly

Large bases are allowed, but structures must not harm server performance or block progression.

  • Avoid excessive foundations, pillars, lights, and unnecessary structures.
  • Do not claim huge areas without actively using them.
  • Abandoned or severely inactive structures may be removed after staff review.
04

Protect Spawn Areas

New players must be able to enter the server safely.

  • Do not build directly on player spawn points.
  • Do not leave aggressive tames or traps near beginner areas.
  • Keep major travel routes reasonably accessible.
05

No Exploits or Duplication

Use only normal gameplay mechanics and approved mods.

  • No item duplication, meshing, rollback abuse, or inventory exploits.
  • Report serious bugs instead of using them for an advantage.
  • Do not use external cheats, macros, or unauthorized software.
06

Fair Trading

Trades must be honest and clearly agreed upon.

  • Do not scam players or change terms after payment.
  • Use screenshots or Discord messages for high-value trades.
  • Staff may review evidence but cannot guarantee replacement in every dispute.
07

Creature and Breeding Management

Manage large creature collections responsibly.

  • Store unused creatures when possible.
  • Avoid leaving large breeding groups active for long periods.
  • Unclaimed babies and abandoned tames may be removed to protect performance.
08

Transfers and Cluster Safety

Use cluster transfers carefully and avoid risky timing.

  • Do not transfer during restart warnings.
  • Verify uploads before leaving a terminal.
  • Report missing survivors or items with the map and approximate time.
09

Names and Content

Player, tribe, creature, and structure names must remain appropriate.

  • No slurs, explicit sexual content, or hateful references.
  • Do not use names intended to impersonate staff.
  • Staff may require inappropriate names or signs to be changed.
10

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff decisions are made to protect the cluster and its community.

  • Do not argue enforcement in global chat.
  • Use Discord tickets to appeal or ask for clarification.
  • Repeated attempts to avoid a ruling may lead to stronger action.
Enforcement

How violations are handled.

The action taken depends on severity, evidence, intent, player history, and the effect on the cluster.

Level Typical Action Examples
Minor Warning or correction Small build issue, accidental obstruction, first-time inappropriate name.
Moderate Structure removal, temporary mute, or temporary ban Repeated rule breaking, griefing, harassment, ignoring staff instructions.
Severe Permanent ban and possible tribe action Cheating, duplication, meshing, serious threats, deliberate server damage.
Player Reports

Give staff useful evidence.

Clear reports are easier to investigate and lead to faster, more accurate decisions.

1

Record the details

Include player or tribe names, map, location, date, and approximate time.

2

Collect evidence

Provide screenshots, video, tribe logs, chat messages, or other supporting information.

3

Open a Discord ticket

Submit the report privately and avoid public arguments while staff investigates.

Need Clarification?

Ask before taking the risk.

Contact staff through Discord when you are unsure whether a build, trade, transfer, or gameplay action is allowed.
